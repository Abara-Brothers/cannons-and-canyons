How to play
One shot per turnNo shot clock — take your time. Firing is what passes the turn.
The weapon stripThe number is ammo left; the little curve beside each icon is that shot’s flight shape.
The draftEvery battle opens with a weapon draft — 5 picks (7 in survival). Cannon and Nuke are standard issue.
Controls tuck awayAfter your first shot the dock tucks itself away — tap the CONTROLS tab to bring it back. FIRE and your aim numbers stay on the sliver.
The camera is yoursPinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan — or use the camera button’s arrows. Shots never yank the view.
The ground is realEvery crater stays. Dig with busters, wall up with Earthworks, or drop a ridge on someone.
