How to play
  • One shot per turn — pick a weapon from the strip; the number is its remaining ammo, and the little curve beside each icon is the shot's flight shape.
  • Camera — pinch or use the zoom buttons; arrows pan the view in all four directions.
  • Duel & Free-for-all — you fight with your 5-weapon Armoury loadout, plus everyone's Tactical Nuke.

Tap a weapon to read what it does.

  • Duel — one on one, last tank standing.
  • Free-for-all — up to four commanders, no teams.
  • Boss Fight — squad up against WARLORD-7. No friendly fire: your shells never hurt your squad.
  • Golf — nine holes of artillery golf; fewest strokes wins. Pick your tee set when you create the game.
  • Alien Invasion — destroy 8 saucers; they return stronger each wave.
  • Zombie Uprising — put down 8 rotting hulks before they reach you.
