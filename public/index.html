How to play

Aiming is a slingshot

  • Touch any empty spot, not the tank — your finger never covers what you are aiming at.
  • Drag away from where you want to hit. The shell flies the opposite way.
  • Pull distance sets power, shown live as a percentage while you hold.
  • Let go to lock it in. Nothing is sent until you press FIRE.

Power, angle and fuel

  • A longer pull is more power — the readout never lies, it is the real number the shot uses.
  • Fine-tune with the steppers. ANGLE nudges one degree at a time, POWER one point.
  • Driving costs fuel, and the fuel bar is your range for this turn.
  • Fuel refills every turn, so repositioning is never a permanent trade.

The ground is a weapon

  • Every crater stays. The map you finish on is not the map you started on.
  • Tanks fall into holes you dig — including your own.
  • Barrels, bunkers and crates are all destructible. Nothing on the field is safe.
  • Fire and gas linger, burning through turns after the shot that started them.
  • Crates drop ammo. Drive into one to open it, or shoot it open from range and deny it.
The ground is realEvery crater stays. Dig with busters, wall up with Earthworks, or drop a ridge on someone.

How a turn works

One shot per turnNo shot clock — take your time. Firing is what passes the turn.
Controls tuck awayBattles open with the dock hidden — tap the CONTROLS tab to raise it. FIRE, your aim numbers and the selected weapon stay on the sliver.
The camera is yoursPinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan — or use the camera button’s arrows. Shots never yank the view.
