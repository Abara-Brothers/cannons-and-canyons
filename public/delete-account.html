Delete your Cannons & Canyons account

You can delete your account and everything attached to it at any time. There are two ways to do it — both delete exactly the same data, immediately and permanently.

1. In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Cannons & Canyons — in the app, or in your browser at the link below.
  2. On the home screen, tap the account chip in the bottom bar.
  3. Choose Delete my account and confirm twice.
Open the game

If you linked a Google account, sign in first so the right account is deleted.

2. By request (if you no longer have the app)

Email us and we will delete the account for you. So we can find the right one, please include:

  • the email address you used to sign in with Google, if you linked one; or
  • your callsign and roughly when you last played, if you played as a guest.
Email support@abarabrothers.com

Or use the support page.

We aim to action requests within 30 days. We may need to confirm you control the account before deleting it.
