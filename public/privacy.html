Privacy Policy — Cannons & Canyons

Made by Abara Brothers · Last updated: 8 August 2026

The short version

Cannons & Canyons is an online artillery game for up to four players. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no chat, and nothing to buy. The game keeps a small cloud save so your progress can follow you between devices, and — only if you turn them on — push subscriptions so we can tell you when it's your turn. You can download everything we hold about you, or delete all of it, from inside the game. We never sell or share your data.

Who is responsible

Abara Brothers is the data controller for the game. You can reach us through our support page.

Your account

Playing creates a guest account: a random identifier with no name, email or password attached. It exists so your progress has somewhere to live. You can optionally link a Google account ("Keep my progress"), which attaches your Google email address to the same identifier so you can sign in from another device. Linking is never required to play.

Turn notifications

Off unless you turn them on. If you enable nudges, your browser or device issues a push subscription — a delivery address plus encryption keys — which we store against your account so notifications work in every future match and on every device you enabled them on. Delivery goes through your platform's push service (operated by Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your device). You can revoke notifications in your browser or device settings at any time; dead subscriptions are deleted automatically.

Crash reports

If the game hits an error, a crash report is sent so we can fix it: the technical error text, where in the code it happened, the game version, and your browser's user-agent string. Crash reports are anonymous — they carry no account identifier, and we do not store your IP address with them. They are deleted automatically after 30 days.

During a match

Your callsign, tank paint, shots, aim and movement are relayed between the players in your room to run the match. Match state lives in server memory only and is discarded when the room ends or times out. Match results are not stored on the server; your own win/loss record is part of your progression, written by your device.

On your device

Your device's local storage holds your callsign, preferences (paint, mute, difficulty, tees), your progression, a match-resume token, and your sign-in session. Local progression is merged with your cloud save; the rest never leaves your device.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or ad SDKs
  • No analytics or third-party trackers
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device files
  • No chat or free-text content between players — callsigns come from the game's own word lists
  • No payment details — the game is free, with nothing to buy

Where your data lives

The game server runs on Render and the database and sign-in service run on Supabase, both hosted in Singapore. Push notifications are delivered by the push service belonging to your platform (Apple, Google or Mozilla). These providers process data on our behalf to run the service and for no other purpose. If you play from the EEA, UK or elsewhere, your data is processed in Singapore as described here.

How long we keep it

Your account and everything attached to it exist until you delete them — deleting your account (below) removes the account, cloud save and push subscriptions immediately, in one action. Match state disappears with the room. Ordinary infrastructure logs (such as connection logs) are short-lived and are not used to build profiles.

Your rights and controls

  • Download my data — in the game's account panel: everything we hold about you, as one JSON file.
  • Delete my account — in the same panel: erases the account, cloud save, push subscriptions and this device's local progress. It cannot be undone.
  • Sign out — any time; your account keeps its progress for your next sign-in.
  • Change your callsign — re-roll it on the title screen whenever you like.
  • You can also ask us to access, correct, restrict or delete your data via the support page, and you have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

The game is suitable for ages 9 and up. It has no chat, no ads, no tracking, and no free-text input — callsigns are picked from the game's own word lists. The only personal data a child's account could contain is an email address, and only if a Google account is deliberately linked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with an updated date. Material changes will be called out in the game.
