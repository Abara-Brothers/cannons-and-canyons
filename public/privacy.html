Privacy Policy — Canyons & Cannons

Made by Abara Brothers · Last updated: 20 July 2026

The short version

Canyons & Cannons is a two-player online artillery game. It has no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no ads SDKs. We collect as close to nothing as a multiplayer game can.

What the game processes

  • Display name — the name you type on the title screen is sent to your opponent so they can see who they're playing. It is held in server memory only for the duration of your match and never written to disk.
  • Gameplay messages — your shots, aim and movement are relayed between you and your opponent to run the match. They are not stored after the match ends.
  • Local preferences — your name, chosen tank paint, mute setting, and a temporary match-resume token are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never leave your device except as described above.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, emails, passwords or phone numbers
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no analytics
  • No contacts, photos, or device files

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences, collects no personal information beyond an optional self-chosen display name, and has no chat facility.

Data retention

Match state lives in server memory only and is deleted when the match ends or times out. There is no database.
