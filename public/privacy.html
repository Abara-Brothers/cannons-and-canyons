Privacy Policy — Cannons & Canyons

Made by Abara Brothers · Last updated: 13 August 2026

The short version

Cannons & Canyons is an online artillery game for up to four players. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no chat, and nothing to buy. The game keeps a small cloud save so your progress can follow you between devices, and — only if you turn them on — push subscriptions so we can tell you when it's your turn. You can download the data attached to your account, or delete all of it, from inside the game. We never sell your data and never share it for anyone else's purposes.

Who is responsible

Abara Brothers (Australia) is the data controller for the game. You can reach us through our support page, which is also the contact point for any request about your data.

Your account

Playing creates a guest account: a random identifier with no name, email or password attached. It exists so your progress has somewhere to live. You can optionally link a Google account ("Keep my progress") so you can sign in from another device. When you do, Google gives us the basic profile information covered by its standard email and profile permissions — your email address, your Google account identifier, your name, and your profile picture — and our sign-in provider stores it against your account. We use the email address to identify you at sign-in; we do not display or otherwise use your Google name or picture in the game. Linking is never required to play.

Turn notifications

Off unless you turn them on. If you enable nudges, your browser or device issues a push subscription — a delivery address plus encryption keys — which we store against your account so notifications work in every future match and on every device you enabled them on. Delivery goes through your platform's push service (operated by Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your device). You can revoke notifications in your browser or device notification settings at any time, which withdraws your consent for future ones. A subscription your push service reports as expired is deleted the next time we try to use it, and every subscription is deleted with your account.

Crash reports

If the game hits an error, a crash report is sent so we can fix it: the technical error text, where in the code it happened, the game version, and your browser's user-agent string. Crash reports are anonymous — they carry no account identifier, and we do not store your IP address with them. They are deleted automatically after 30 days.

During a match

Your callsign, tank paint, shots, aim and movement are relayed between the players in your room to run the match. Match state lives in server memory only and is discarded when the room ends or times out. Match results are not stored on the server; your own win/loss record is part of your progression, written by your device.

On your device

Your device's local storage holds your callsign, preferences (paint, mute, difficulty, tees), your progression, a match-resume token, and your sign-in session. Your callsign and progression are also sent to us as described above, and your sign-in session is exchanged with our sign-in provider; your preferences and resume token stay on your device.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or ad SDKs
  • No analytics or third-party trackers
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing of it for anyone else's purposes — the providers named below act only on our instructions
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device files
  • No chat or free-text content between players — callsigns come from the game's own word lists
  • No payment details — the game is free, with nothing to buy

Where your data lives, and transfers

The game server runs on Render and the database and sign-in service run on Supabase, both hosted in Singapore. Push notifications are delivered by the push service belonging to your platform (Apple, Google or Mozilla). These providers are our processors: they handle the data to run the service on our behalf and for no other purpose.

Singapore is not covered by a UK or European Commission adequacy decision, so if you play from the EEA or UK your data is transferred outside that area. Those transfers are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum where UK law applies), which are incorporated into our data-processing agreements with both Render and Supabase. You can ask us for details of these safeguards through the support page.

How long we keep it

Your account and everything attached to it exist until you delete them — deleting your account (below) removes the account, cloud save, sign-in identity and push subscriptions immediately, in one action. Crash reports are deleted after 30 days. Match state disappears with the room. Ordinary infrastructure logs (such as connection logs) are short-lived and are not used to build profiles.

Your rights and controls

  • Download my data — in the game's account panel: your account identifier, linked email (if any), callsign, cloud save and this device's progression, as one JSON file. For anything held about you that is not in that file — such as your stored push subscriptions or the profile details Google returned at sign-in — ask us through the support page and we will provide it.
  • Delete my account — in the same panel: erases the account, cloud save, sign-in identity, push subscriptions and this device's local progress. It cannot be undone. If you no longer have the app, you can request the same deletion at this page.
  • Correct or restrict — ask us through the support page.
  • Object — to the crash reporting we run on legitimate interests, at any time.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off notifications in your browser or device settings; withdrawing does not affect notifications already sent.
  • Sign out — any time; your account keeps its progress for your next sign-in.
  • Change your callsign — re-roll it on the title screen whenever you like.
  • You have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. In Australia that is the OAIC; in the EEA or UK it is your national supervisory authority.

You do not have to give us anything to play: a guest account is created automatically and needs nothing from you. Linking Google is optional — without it, your progress simply stays on the device you played on. There is no automated decision-making or profiling of any kind in the game.

Children

The game is suitable for ages 9 and up. It has no chat, no ads, no tracking, and no free-text input — callsigns are picked from the game's own word lists. A guest account holds no personal details at all. If a Google account is linked, that account's email address, Google identifier, name and profile picture are stored as described above — so if your child plays, we suggest leaving the game on its guest account, and you can delete everything at any time from the account panel or the deletion page.
